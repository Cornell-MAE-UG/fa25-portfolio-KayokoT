In Problem Set #4 in MAE 3240: Heat Transfer, the first problem applied concepts such as thermal resistance networks and solar irradiation to realistically model a photovoltaic panels. It was particularly insightful in demonstrating how electrical efficiency degrades as the panel temperature increases. Promoting heat transfer away from the panel is therefore an important thermal management challenge when designing more efficient solar energy systems. Understanding the underlying governing equations and thermal resistance model for this system will be especially useful as I continue working in the Energy Research Lab to optimize and quantify the performance of a passive PV cooling system.
