Our group dissected and analyzed the fluid-mechanical components of a standard hair dryer. I was responsible for synthesizing and validating the relevant assumptions (incompressible, inviscid flow) which allowed us to use actuator disk theory and Bernoulli's equation to explain the fluid's acceleration. I also took measurements and plotted the speed of the flow at different settings, allowing us to determine that the heat did not significantly affect the flow.
