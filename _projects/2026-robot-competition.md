For MAE 3780, I worked in a team of three to create a robot that navigated a competition board to collect cubes. My primary responsibility was creating, testing, and debugging the C code. I also wired components, ordered parts, and helped build the robot. We placed third in the round robin, and unfortunately did not advance. I gained critical skills in building electrical circuits, programming a microcontroller, and debugging. Our final report is linked below.
